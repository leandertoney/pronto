What changed and why, mapped to the audit: The 3-bar mark that already sits on your Home screen becomes the whole brand: static on Home, animating as an equalizer above the mic while the VAD listens (turning "always listening" from invisible to felt, the single biggest trust gap in a hands-free app). Typography does the premium work the palette can't do alone: Bricolage Grotesque for display and Spanish phrases, Instrument Sans for body, replacing bare system font. The why box gets promoted from an afterthought to the score card's centerpiece, since word-level hit/miss chips are a feature none of your competitors show. The choosing card finally advertises the spoken commands on screen ("or just say: continúa"), fixing the discoverability problem of a voice feature currently taught by a one-time audio tip. Navigation chrome goes English (My phrases, My progress) per the gringo rule, while all learning content stays Spanish-first. Emoji survives only where it's expressive (celebrations, chips); structural chrome gets real icons. No em dashes anywhere in the copy.
