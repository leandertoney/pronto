Design logic: the tracker's headline celebrates presence ("You showed up 4 days"), the empty days are neutral cream rather than broken chains, and today is a dashed outline, an invitation instead of a debt. The month grid uses turquoise depth for minutes talked, so consistency LOOKS like water filling in, no fire emoji anywhere. The "getting rusty" card is the tracker's teeth: instead of guilting you about yesterday, it points at a real phrase going stale and gives a one-tap way back into the conversation. The dictionary adds three things the chip pile couldn't do: search, a strength bar per word (reusing the score band colors), and provenance, every word shows the phrase it came from, reinforcing that vocabulary here comes from YOUR life, not a word list.
